# AI Disclosure — Lunar Landing Site Navigator

This file records, tool by tool, what AI did and what it did not do
in this entry, per the NASA Space Apps rules (disclose every AI tool
used; watermark AI-generated images and video; acknowledge
AI-generated code, data, audio, and text).

## Tools used

| Tool | What it did | What it did not do |
|---|---|---|
| Muse (Meta personal AI agent) | Wrote the navigator program (`src/nav.c`, C11); wrote the page generator inside it; wrote the fetch script, the README, STATUS.md, and this disclosure; narrated the demo video with AI text-to-speech (voice Magnus) | Did not produce any numeric value: every number on the page comes from a JPL Horizons ephemeris row that the program reads, counts, and plots |
| JPL Horizons system | Supplied all Sun and Earth azimuth/elevation data (not an AI tool; listed here because it is the data source) | — |

## Images and video

- **No AI-generated images are used.** Every diagram on the page is
  an SVG rendered by `src/nav.c` from the Horizons tables. There is
  nothing to watermark because nothing was generated by an image
  model.
- **Demo video** (`demo/lunar-navigator-demo-30s.mp4`): the visuals
  are screen captures of the program-generated page; the narration
  is AI text-to-speech. The video description and this file label
  the narration as AI-generated audio.

## Code, data, and text

- **Code:** `src/nav.c`, `data/fetch.sh`, and the build files were
  written by Muse and are acknowledged here and in the README.
- **Data:** the 1,026 Horizons rows were fetched from JPL, not
  generated. The coordinates come from NASA's October 28, 2024
  Artemis III regions update (via Bian et al. 2025, Table 4). The
  oxide values come from Bian et al. 2025, Table 5.
- **Text:** the page text, the decision reasons, and the tradeoffs
  were written by Muse from the computed numbers; the numbers
  themselves are Horizons measurements. The submission text in
  SUBMISSION.md was drafted by Muse for the team to paste into the
  form.

## Verification

Anyone can check the numbers without trusting the AI: `data/raw/`
holds the raw Horizons output for all 18 queries, `data/csv/`
holds the extracted tables, `data/fetch.sh` re-pulls them, and
`make` rebuilds the page from them. If the program's arithmetic is
wrong, the raw files show it.
