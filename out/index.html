How the numbers were made

Region coordinates are NASA's October 28, 2024 update of the nine Artemis III candidate regions, as tabulated in Table 4 of the 1D-CNN preprint (Preprints.org, doi:10.20944/preprints202511.1488.v1 — preprint, not peer-reviewed) and cross-checked against the USGS ScienceBase values (agreement within about 1°, the regions themselves are roughly 10–38 km across).

Sun and Earth positions come from the JPL Horizons system, observer ephemeris, https://ssd.jpl.nasa.gov/api/horizons.api (Horizons API v1.3). Each query: target Sun (COMMAND='10') or Earth (COMMAND='399'), center coord@301 with the region's longitude/latitude/0 km, QUANTITIES='4' (airless apparent azimuth and elevation, degrees), 2026-11-15 to 2026-11-29, every 6 hours — 57 rows per query, 18 queries, 1,026 rows total. The raw Horizons output is kept in data/raw/; the extracted tables in data/csv/; the fetch script is data/fetch.sh, so anyone can re-pull the same numbers.

Limits, stated plainly: elevations are the target's center above a flat horizon — the Sun's and Earth's angular radii (about 0.27° and 0.95°) are not added, and no local terrain is modeled. At these grazing angles a nearby ridge changes the real illumination, so the terrain guard is UNKNOWN until a LOLA horizon profile is computed. The 6-hour sampling is interpolated linearly for rise/set times; the true crossing can differ by minutes. Nothing on this page is a landing recommendation — it is a comparison of sky geometry, and the decision column says which site wins that comparison.

Use of AI

This entry was built by Jessica Williams' team with Muse (Meta's personal AI agent) as the AI tool: Muse wrote the navigator program, generated this page from the Horizons data, and narrated the demo video. All numeric values come from JPL Horizons ephemeris rows, not from the AI — the program only reads, counts, and plots them. No AI-generated images are used: every diagram is rendered by the program from the measured data. The demo video's narration is AI text-to-speech and is labeled as such.
