At the Moon's south pole, the Sun never rises high. It circles the horizon, a few degrees up at best. We pulled real Sun and Earth positions from JPL Horizons for all nine Artemis Three candidate sites, across fourteen days. Mons Mouton Plateau wins: the Sun never sets, and Earth never drops below eleven degrees. Peak near Cabeus B has the highest Sun — but loses half the window to darkness. Every number traces to a Horizons row.
